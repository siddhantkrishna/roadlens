# Verification fixtures

- `bus.jpg`: real bus photograph from
  [Ultralytics](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/blob/main/ultralytics/assets/bus.jpg),
  downloaded 2026-10-29. Upstream repository: AGPL-3.0. Used for a high-confidence
  bus detection assertion, not as a camera stream.
- `traffic.avi`: short excerpt of Intel's real
  [car-detection.mp4](https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos/blob/master/car-detection.mp4),
  frames 94–105, re-encoded as Motion JPEG at 12 FPS for compact, offline tests. The
  [source repository](https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos) is CC BY 4.0
  licensed; see `INTEL-LICENSE.txt`.

Fixtures are for verification. Production inputs always come from actual
AVFoundation/OpenCV cameras or a user-supplied video file.

`traffic.scene.json` defines a finite line at normalized y=0.16 and a road zone
specific to this clip. `traffic.annotations.json` records the visually reviewed
car crossing with direction/time bounds and hashes of the video/scene. No
independently measured ground-plane calibration is available; speed is therefore
unavailable. Reproduce the crossing check with `roadlens validate-footage` as
documented in [TRAFFIC.md](../TRAFFIC.md).

## Public release policy

Only these two reviewed media files are included. Their exact SHA-256 values and
sources are recorded in [docs/public-assets.json](../docs/public-assets.json).
Ignore rules reject other photographs/videos by default; the release gate also
rejects forced-in private media. Do not replace a fixture with camera footage.

The three screenshots under `docs/images/` are actual Qt captures over the Intel
fixture, generated by `python scripts/capture_screenshots.py` after model setup.
They contain no personal feed, device ID or home path. Original UI text/artwork
is AGPL-3.0-only; the underlying modified Intel footage remains CC BY 4.0. Preserve
this attribution when reusing screenshots. Regenerated images require visual
privacy review and manifest hash updates before release checks can pass.
